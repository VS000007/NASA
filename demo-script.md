# 3-Minute Demo Script

> **Space Archaeologists: Abandoned but Not Forgotten**
> Interactive storytelling about NASA's discarded equipment on the Moon and Mars.

---

## 0:00 – 0:20 | Solar System View (Opening)

*[Page loads to animated solar system with orbiting planets]*

> "Welcome to Space Archaeologists! You're looking at a real-time solar system built with data from NASA's JPL Horizons system. Notice the Moon orbiting Earth, and Mars out here in its wider orbit. See those targeting brackets? Those mark bodies with abandoned hardware — **47 relics on the Moon** and **19 on Mars**. Let's explore. Click the Moon."

## 0:20 – 0:50 | Moon Surface Map

*[Warp transition → Leaflet map with NASA Moon Trek tiles]*

> "We've descended onto a high-resolution NASA Moon Trek mosaic. Each pin represents a landing site with abandoned equipment. Notice three types of markers: **rockets for landing sites**, **pins for last known positions**, and **explosions for impact sites**."

*[Drag timeline slider from 1966 to 1972]*

> "This timeline starts in 1966 with Surveyor 1 — the first US soft landing. Watch the markers appear as we advance through the Apollo era. By 1972, Apollo 17 leaves the final human footprints."

*[Click the Apollo 11 pin cluster]*

> "Apollo 11's Tranquility Base has multiple artifacts — the descent stage, the EASEP science package, and the ascent stage whose location is actually unknown. It may still be orbiting the Moon!"

## 0:50 – 1:30 | Object Viewer — Apollo 11

*[Click 'Inspect' on Apollo 11 Descent Stage]*

> "Here's the interactive 3D model — a real NASA model you can rotate and zoom. Click these numbered hotspots to learn about each component: the landing legs, the peace plaque."

*[Toggle reading level]*

> "We have two reading levels — **Explorer** for ages 7-10, and **Commander** for 11-16. Watch how the story adapts."

*[Click 'Listen to Story']*

> "Voice narration reads the story aloud. Perfect for young readers."

*[Click the archive photo → caption panel opens]*

> "Every image has a caption panel showing the NASA image ID, credit line, source link, and alt text. Full attribution."

*[Click 'Fly to It']*

> "This opens NASA Eyes on the Solar System — the official 3D view."

## 1:30 – 2:10 | Mars Map

*[Navigate back → Switch to Mars]*

> "Mars has 19 pieces of hardware. Here's Curiosity at Gale Crater — still active since 2012. And over in Jezero Crater, Perseverance and Ingenuity."

*[Click Opportunity rover]*

> "Opportunity drove 45 kilometers from its landing site to Perseverance Valley. Its marker shows 'last known position' — where it went silent during a dust storm in 2018. The archaeological field notes tell the whole story."

*[Show the search box — type 'Viking']*

> "Real-time search filters the map. Category pills let you focus on just rovers, or impactors, or entry hardware like heat shields."

## 2:10 – 2:40 | Accessibility & Table View

*[Click 📋 List View button]*

> "For accessibility, every map has a full table alternative. Keyboard users can tab through all 19 Mars artifacts, press Enter to view any one. We support screen readers, skip-nav links, and prefers-reduced-motion."

## 2:40 – 3:00 | Credits & Close

*[Navigate to /credits.html]*

> "The Credits page auto-generates from our dataset — every image credited to NASA and the mission team, with links to the NASA Image Library."

*[Return to solar system view]*

> "66 artifacts. Two worlds. Every piece verified against NASA archives. Abandoned, but not forgotten."

---

**Total runtime: ~3 minutes**
